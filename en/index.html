FrontDesk V1

What FrontDesk does

Qualifies every incoming enquiry

  • Answers within seconds, evenings and weekends included.
  • Asks the right questions based on the project: buying, renting or valuation.
  • Gathers budget, timeline, number of occupants and availability.
  • Sends you a clear summary of every contact by email.
  • Sends an SMS confirmation.

Offers and confirms a viewing appointment

  • Offers slots based on your real calendar.
  • Confirms the viewing and adds it straight to your calendar.
  • Avoids double bookings.

You stay in control

  • You set the rules: listings, hours, tone.
  • You receive every qualified contact with its history.
  • When a request falls outside the rules, FrontDesk hands it back to you instead of improvising.
Use cases

Built for estate agencies

Qualifying a rental enquiry

FrontDesk identifies the budget, desired move-in date and number of occupants before passing on the contact.

I'm looking for a 2-bedroom, max budget €950.
Noted. When would you be available to move in?

Qualifying a buying enquiry

FrontDesk identifies the budget, the type of property sought and the project timeline before passing on the contact.

Hi, I'm looking for a 3-façade house, max budget €320,000.
Noted. Do you already have financing in place, or is that still to be arranged?

Qualifying a valuation request

FrontDesk identifies the property type, its location and the reason for the request before passing on the contact.

Hi, I'd like to get my flat valued for a sale.
Of course. What's the address, and when are you planning to sell?

FAQ

Before you get started

What does FrontDesk actually do in V1?

Two things: qualify every incoming call (project type, budget, availability) and offer a viewing appointment based on your real availability.

Is it suited to a small independent agency?

Yes, FrontDesk is built for independent agencies of 2 to 10 people, anywhere in Belgium.

Is my data protected?

Your calls are processed by ElevenLabs (voice processing, a US-based company), then orchestrated by n8n, hosted on our own Hostinger server. You stay in control: only the information needed for qualification and booking is processed. [TO BE COMPLETED: exact retention period, legal basis for the ElevenLabs non-EU transfer, to confirm before publication]

Is there a minimum commitment?

No, FrontDesk runs on a one-month notice period, with no long-term commitment.

Will my customers know they're talking to an assistant?

Yes, FrontDesk introduces itself as your agency's virtual assistant from the start of the call.

What happens when FrontDesk doesn't know how to answer?

It passes the request on to you rather than improvising an answer.

Which channels and tools does it work with?

FrontDesk handles your phone calls. It works with Google Calendar (tested and confirmed); your needs (other calendar, other tools) are assessed before set-up.

How long does set-up take?

A few days.
